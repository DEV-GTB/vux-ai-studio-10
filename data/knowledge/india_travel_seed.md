# India Travel Knowledge Seed

## India overview
India (Bharat) is a federal parliamentary democratic republic with 28 states and 8 union territories. Capital: New Delhi. Currency: Indian Rupee (INR). Major languages include Hindi, English and 22 scheduled languages. Iconic landmarks: Taj Mahal (Agra), Red Fort and India Gate (Delhi), Qutub Minar, Gateway of India (Mumbai), Charminar (Hyderabad), Golden Temple (Amritsar), Hawa Mahal (Jaipur), Mysore Palace, Sun Temple (Konark), Meenakshi Amman Temple (Madurai), Statue of Unity (Gujarat). National emergency number is 112. Tourism brand: Incredible India.

## State nicknames
Kerala: God's Own Country, Land of Spices, Land of Backwaters. Tamil Nadu: Land of Temples. Karnataka: One State, Many Worlds. Gujarat: Jewel of Western India, Land of Legends. Andhra Pradesh: described as Rice Bowl region; Hyderabad City of Pearls. Goa: Pearl of the Orient. Rajasthan: Land of Kings. Punjab: Land of Five Rivers. Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand: Dev Bhoomi. Jammu and Kashmir: Paradise on Earth. Meghalaya: Abode of Clouds. Assam: Land of Tea. Sikkim: Land of Monasteries.

## Kerala
Kerala lies on the south-western coast along the Arabian Sea and Western Ghats. Capital: Thiruvananthapuram. Language: Malayalam. Known for literacy (~96%), spices, rubber. 14 districts: Thiruvananthapuram, Kollam, Pathanamthitta, Alappuzha, Kottayam, Idukki, Ernakulam, Thrissur, Palakkad, Malappuram, Kozhikode, Wayanad, Kannur, Kasaragod. Signature places: Kochi (Chinese fishing nets, Fort Kochi), Alappuzha backwaters, Munnar tea plantations and Eravikulam National Park, Thekkady/Periyar, Kovalam and Varkala beaches, Athirappilly Falls (Niagara of India), Silent Valley National Park (Palakkad), Sabarimala pilgrimage, Wayanad misty hills and Edakkal Caves, Bekal Fort (Kasaragod), Kannur Theyyam rituals. Festivals: Onam, Vishu, Thrissur Pooram, Theyyam season, Attukal Pongala. Art forms: Kathakali, Mohiniyattam, Theyyam, Koodiyattam (UNESCO), Kalaripayattu. Food: Kerala Sadya, appam with stew, puttu and kadala curry, Kerala parotta, fish molee, Malabar biryani. Islam arrived early via Arab traders; Cheraman Juma Mosque in Kodungallur is widely regarded as India's first mosque.

## Tamil Nadu
Tamil Nadu is on India's south-eastern peninsula with a long coastline, river plains and hills including the Nilgiris. Heritage includes Tamil literature, Dravidian temple architecture, Bharatanatyam, Carnatic music. Places: Chennai (Marina Beach, Kapaleeshwarar Temple), Mahabalipuram (Pallava rock-cut monuments), Kanchipuram (temples, silk), Thanjavur (Brihadisvara Temple), Madurai (Meenakshi Amman Temple), Rameswaram, Kanyakumari, Ooty and Coonoor hill stations, Courtallam waterfalls. Tamil Nadu should not be represented only through temples; include archaeology, ecology, industry, cinema, science, agriculture, ports, urban development.

## Karnataka
Karnataka combines the Deccan Plateau, Western Ghats and a long Arabian Sea coastline. Heritage: Kannada literature, Hoysala and Vijayanagara architecture, Wodeyar of Mysuru, Yakshagana. Places: Bengaluru (IT, Lalbagh, Cubbon Park), Mysuru (Mysore Palace, Dasara), Hampi (Vijayanagara ruins), Chikkamagaluru coffee country, Coorg, Gokarna beaches, Jog Falls, Murudeshwar, Dandeli. Food: Mysore pak, Udupi cuisine, Mangalorean coastal food, North Karnataka jolada rotti, Kodava cuisine, Dharwad peda.

## Gujarat
Gujarat is in western India with coastline, Rann of Kutch salt flats, dryland ecosystems, historic ports, stepwells, temples and industrial cities. Heritage: Harappan archaeology (Dholavira), maritime trade, Jain heritage, stepwells. Places: Ahmedabad (Sabarmati Ashram, Sidi Saiyyed Mosque, Adalaj Stepwell), Vadodara (Laxmi Vilas Palace), Patan (Rani ki Vav), Modhera (Sun Temple), Bhuj and the Great Rann of Kutch, Gir (Asiatic lions), Somnath, Dwarka, Junagadh/Girnar, Surat. Food: Gujarati thali, Kathiyawadi cuisine, dhokla, thepla, undhiyu, farsan. Diu is a neighbouring union territory, keep records separate.

## Andhra Pradesh
Andhra Pradesh lies on the south-eastern coast with a long coastline, river deltas, plains, hills, forests, religious centres, Buddhist archaeological sites, ports. Places: Visakhapatnam (RK Beach, Araku Valley, Borra Caves), Vijayawada (Kanaka Durga Temple), Amaravati (Buddhist heritage), Rajahmundry, Tirupati and Tirumala, Gandikota gorge, Belum Caves, Horsley Hills, Konaseema delta. Food: coastal Andhra cuisine, Rayalaseema cuisine, gongura, pulihora. Distinguish Andhra Pradesh from Telangana.

## Travel services guidance
Accommodation: never fabricate booking URLs; verify operating status, prices, taxes and amenities before recommending; use verified official/booking sources. Petrol pumps: use IndianOil locator (locator.iocl.com) and other official fuel company locators; do not assume 24-hour service or all fuels. Hospitals: verify departments and emergency services; do not infer capability from name; emergency number 112. Viewpoints and waterfalls: access, weather, safety and permits vary; do not advise swimming without verified safety guidance. Seasonality: Kerala monsoon creates landslide/flood risks; check current warnings and road status.

## Festival date awareness
Festival dates vary by lunar calendar and local/official scheduling; do not assign permanent dates. State exact dates only with verified current source.

## Less-publicized (hidden gem) categories
Use explicit labels: less widely publicized attraction, local community attraction, offbeat heritage site, quiet natural landscape, seasonal scenic destination, lesser-known food destination, small museum, village tourism destination, low-crowd alternative (if evidence), remote location needing planning. Always explain evidence, access, permissions and safety.
